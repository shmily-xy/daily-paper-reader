## 摘要
我们介绍了一项在实验室规模的3升搅拌槽反应器（STR）中进行的混合动力学实验研究，该反应器配备有两个拉什顿涡轮和三个挡板。利用时间分辨的四维粒子追踪测速技术，我们成功捕获了反应器全容积内多达40,000个示踪粒子的轨迹，尽管搅拌器和挡板造成障碍，仍提供了前所未有的时间分辨流场和混合信息。基于这些拉格朗日数据，我们分析了速度、加速度和空间扩散，揭示了各向异性混合。通过采用基于网络的新型分析方法对实验粒子轨迹进行处理，我们识别出具有强烈内部混合但与相邻区域交换较弱的连贯流体区域。我们发现五个不同的区域作为传输屏障，对化学和生物化工过程中的底物分布具有重要影响。因此，我们的方法实现并拓展了丹克沃茨和莱文斯皮尔的早期思想实验，通过提供对单个流体微团行为以及化学和生物反应器中拉格朗日混合的详细洞察，为化学和生物化工过程的评估与优化提供了有价值的途径。这些轨迹数据已免费公开，可供进一步研究作为实验参考。

## Abstract
We present an experimental investigation of mixing dynamics within a laboratory-scale 3-liter stirred tank reactor (STR) equipped with two Rushton turbines and three baffles. Using time-resolved, four-dimensional particle tracking velocimetry, we successfully capture trajectories of up to 40,000 tracer particles in the full reactor volume despite obstructions by stirrer and baffles, providing unprecedented time-resolved flow and mixing information. From these Lagrangian data, we analyze velocities, accelerations, and spatial dispersion, revealing anisotropic mixing. By utilizing novel network-based analysis methods on the experimental particle trajectories, we identify coherent fluid compartments that exhibit strong internal mixing but weak exchange with neighboring compartments. We uncover five distinct compartments acting as transport barriers, which have a high impact on substrate distribution in chemical and biochemical processes. Our approach thus realizes and extends early thought experiments from Danckwerts and Levenspiel by providing detailed insight into the behavior of single fluid parcels and Lagrangian mixing withing chemical and biochemical reactors, offering a valuable approach for evaluation and optimization of chemical and biochemical processes. The trajectory data are made freely available to serve as an experimental reference for further research.
