## 摘要
本书有一个首要目标：让您从学习胶体科学的基础原理，迅速过渡到在实验室中设计具有功能或运动的胶体系统。本书无意提供关于胶体与表面科学所有方面的百科式知识；相反，我选择不纳入许多确实重要的主题。为了达成既定目标，我采用两条并行的策略。首先，我给出可以立即使用的实用结果。如果存在繁琐的计算，我已努力将其重构为表格或图形，例如哈梅克常数和椭球体的流体动力学。其次，我描述各种胶体现象的物理原理，以便您作为研究人员能够思考替代策略。在平衡算法与解释之间，我提供即插即用的例题，使您熟悉单位、常数和典型值。练习题则对最基础的理论进行拓展，开辟新的可能性，同时提供在实践和研究中实用的结果。在整本书中，我提供了我们常用于黏度、zeta电位、离子现象及其他参数的数据。我没有提供所有参考文献和技术，而是提供了我们实验室最常用的那些。最后，本书的目标不是对胶体进行详尽无遗的研究，而是成为尽可能快速制备所需胶体系统的入门之道。

## Abstract
This book has one primary goal: To get you moving quickly from learning basic principles of colloid science, to designing colloidal systems for function or motion in the lab. It is not intended to provide encyclopedic knowledge about all aspects of colloid and surface science; rather, I have chosen not to include many topics of genuine importance. In reaching the intended goal, I use two parallel strategies. First, I give practical results that can be used immediately. If there is a cumbersome calculation, I have worked to reframe it into a table or figure. This is the case for Hamaker constants and hydrodynamics of spheroids, for instance. Second, I describe the physics of various colloidal phenomena, so that you as a researcher can think about alternative strategies.   In balancing algorithms with explanations, I provide plug-and-chug example problems to familiarize you with units, constants, and typical values. The practice problems provide extensions to the most basic theory that open new possibilities, while also giving results that are useful in practical and research studies. Throughout the book, I provide data we commonly use for viscosities, zeta potentials, ionic phenomena, and other parameters. Rather than providing every reference and every technique, I have provided those references and techniques that we most often use in our lab. In the end, this book aims not to be an exhaustive study of colloids, but rather to be a doorway to producing desired colloidal systems as quickly as possible.
